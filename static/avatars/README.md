# Built-in portraits

These thirty-two fictional portraits were generated with an image-generation
tool, then exported as 384 × 384 JPEG. They depict no real person, and no
third-party image was used as a source.

The eighteen general-purpose portraits comprise nine feminine and nine masculine
faces.

Shared prompt, summarised: a coherent series of fictional adults aged 23 to 29,
young, attractive, charismatic and self-assured in a sensual but elegant fashion
register; semi-realistic editorial portraits with a light gouache texture,
head-and-shoulders framing legible at 32 px, warm lighting and plum/amber
backgrounds matching Praxis, with no nudity, text, logo or celebrity likeness.

The two complementary series were produced as 3 × 2 sheets: women on the top
row, men on the bottom row. The first varies origins, skin tones and hairstyles
(long hair, short afro, copper shag, red curls, shaved head and shoulder-length
hair). The second adds a platinum pixie, long red waves, an East Asian bob,
short locs, a blond buzz cut and glasses. Style, framing and palette were locked
to the original series to preserve visual consistency.

Four role portraits use a brighter palette with the same editorial finish:
`cast-19.jpg` (police officer, blue/cyan), `cast-20.jpg` (robot, magenta/cyan),
`cast-21.jpg` (scientist, green/yellow) and `cast-22.jpg` (artist,
orange/purple). They also serve the ready-made character templates offered in
the interface.

Ten further variants come in feminine/masculine pairs: doctors (`cast-23` and
`cast-24`), firefighters (`cast-25` and `cast-26`), police officers (`cast-27`
and `cast-28`), artists (`cast-29` and `cast-30`) and adult students (`cast-31`
and `cast-32`). They reuse the same head-and-shoulders framing, gouache texture
and colour blocking as the earlier role series.
